# Image sources, credits and reuse terms

All images appear as editorial location context or illustrative concept artwork. None depicts an available property, verified listing, particular home for sale, price sample or investment return. Visible captions and credits are kept off the photographs; their source, creator, licensing and concept-art disclosures are collected at `/image-credits/`. Descriptive alt text remains on the images for assistive technology.

## Integrity and display rules

- Keep creator, source, license and adaptation information linked from `/image-credits/`.
- Retain descriptive, truthful alt text on every image; alt text is not displayed as a caption.
- Disclose all AI-generated imagery as concept art, not a real photograph or property listing, on the central Image Credits page.
- Never use search-result thumbnails, watermarked previews, Dreamstime, Shutterstock or Alamy assets without a separate valid licence.
- Keep these source and license links intact when moving or replacing an image. For ShareAlike images, preserve the listed ShareAlike license for the adapted image file.
