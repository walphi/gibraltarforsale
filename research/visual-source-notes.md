## Unsplash official license

Official source: https://unsplash.com/license. Restrictions include selling images without significant modification and compiling Unsplash images to replicate a similar or competing service. This property guide uses individual editorial images, not an image-collection service.

## Exclusions and integrity

Do not use Dreamstime, Shutterstock or Alamy search-result images, watermarked Unsplash+/stock preview files, or any rights-unclear result. Some locally downloaded Unsplash search-preview files include visible Unsplash+ watermarks even though nearby results are free; those preview files are not suitable for publication. Prefer the exact free Unsplash source page and original image URL or the official Commons original. Do not imply a photograph or generated concept shows an available Gibraltar property. Label AI-generated residential scenes as illustrative inspiration, not a listing.
