# Gibraltar Property for Sale: How to Find & Verify Listings in 2027

Finding property in Gibraltar is different from larger markets: there is no single MLS, no centralised portal with comprehensive coverage, and a significant portion of transactions occur off-market or through local networks. This guide explains where to look, how to verify what you find, and the red flags that should prompt deeper investigation.

## Where Gibraltar Properties Are Listed

### Primary Online Portals

| Portal | Coverage | Strengths | Limitations |
|--------|----------|-----------|-------------|
| **Century 21 Gibraltar** | ~40% of open market | Established agency; good marina coverage | Agent-only listings |
| **Chestertons Gibraltar** | ~25% | Prime / super-prime focus | High-end bias |
| **Savills Gibraltar** | ~15% | International buyer network | Limited mid-market |
| **Gibraltar Property Shop** | ~15% | Local resale & rental | Basic search tools |
| **Rightmove Overseas** | Aggregated | Familiar UK interface | Incomplete; delayed updates |
| **Facebook Groups** | Informal | Off-market; rental leads | Unverified; scams exist |

**No single portal has full coverage.** Serious buyers should monitor all of the above and register alerts with 3–4 agencies directly.

### Off-Market Channels (Estimated 30–40% of Transactions)

1. **Direct agent relationships** — Local agents maintain buyer registries; register with 3–4 before you need to buy
2. **Solicitor networks** — Gibraltar solicitors often know of probate, divorce or corporate sales before listing
3. **Developer direct** — New phases (e.g., Midtown, Westside Gardens) often sell 50%+ pre-launch to registered buyers
4. **Expat / community networks** — Word-of-mouth in schools, clubs, workplace communities

## Verifying a Listing: 7-Step Checklist

### 1. Confirm the Agent's Credentials
- Check **Gibraltar Real Estate Agents Register** (maintained by the Office of Fair Trading)
- Ask for **Professional Indemnity Insurance** certificate
- Verify **OFT licence number** on correspondence
- Established agencies: Century 21, Chestertons, Savills, GTS, Property Shop, Elysium, JYS

### 2. Verify the Property Exists & Matches the Description
- Request **full address** (not just area) — vague locations ("South District apartment") are a red flag
- Cross-reference with **Land Registry title plan** (your solicitor can obtain pre-offer)
- Check **Google Street View / satellite** for building context, access, outlook
- Ask for **recent photos** (within 30 days) — staged photos from years ago are common

### 3. Confirm Tenure & Category
- **Open Market vs Restricted** — Request written confirmation from agent
- **Leasehold details** — Unexpired term, ground rent, freeholder, consent requirements
- **Service charge** — Current annual charge, 3-year history, sinking fund balance, planned major works

### 4. Check Planning & Building Control History
- Use **Gibraltar e-Planning portal** (public search by address)
- Look for: enforcement notices, refused applications, conditions not discharged
- Ask agent for **completion certificate** (new builds) or **regularisation certificates** (alterations)

### 5. Verify Price Against Comparable Evidence
- Ask agent for **3 recent comparable sales** (not asking prices) in same development/area
- Check **Land Registry price paid data** (limited availability in Gibraltar; your solicitor can access)
- Be wary of **"offers over"** pricing — often indicates no firm valuation

### 6. Confirm Vacant Possession & Tenancy Status
- Is the property **vacant, tenanted, or occupied by owner**?
- If tenanted: request **tenancy agreement**, rent schedule, deposit details, notice status
- Gibraltar tenancy law (Landlord & Tenant Act) gives sitting tenants significant protections

### 7. Request the Sales Pack Before Offering
A complete sales pack should include:
- [ ] Title documents / lease
- [ ] 3 years service charge accounts + budget
- [ ] Building insurance schedule
- [ ] Management company accounts
- [ ] Planning permissions / building regs for alterations
- [ ] EPC (Energy Performance Certificate)
- [ ] Asbestos survey (pre-2000 buildings)
- [ ] Fire risk assessment (common parts)

**Do not offer without reviewing the sales pack.** Agents who pressure you to offer "before someone else does" without providing documentation are not acting in your interest.

## Red Flags: When to Walk Away (or Dig Deeper)

| Red Flag | Why It Matters | Action |
|----------|----------------|--------|
| Agent unwilling to provide sales pack | May hide defects, lease issues, service charge problems | Instruct solicitor to request directly |
| "Cash buyers only" on mortgageable property | Suggests structural/lease/title defect | Full structural survey + title investigation |
| Significant discount to "market value" | Often indicates hidden defect or lease issue | Independent RICS valuation |
| No Land Registry title number provided | Property may not be registered or have title defects | Solicitor to investigate pre-offer |
| Service charge arrears >6 months | Management issues; potential litigation | Request management accounts; consider walking away |
| Freeholder consent "usually granted" | Not a guarantee; can be withheld or delayed | Get written consent pre-exchange |
| Listing photos don't match address | Misrepresentation; possible bait-and-switch | Verify address independently; report to OFT |

## Practical Search Strategy

1. **Register with 4 agencies** — Specify: budget, areas, property type, tenure preference, timeline
2. **Set up portal alerts** — Daily email alerts on all 4 portals with your criteria
3. **Monitor e-Planning weekly** — New applications in your target areas signal upcoming supply
4. **Engage a buying agent (optional)** — 1–2% fee; accesses off-market; negotiates on your behalf
5. **View efficiently** — Batch viewings by area; bring checklist; photograph everything
6. **Verify before offering** — Sales pack → solicitor review → survey → mortgage offer → offer

## Off-Market Opportunity: How to Access

1. **Tell your solicitor you're buying** — They hear of probate/corporate sales early
2. **Register with developers** — Midtown, Westside Gardens, future Eastside phases
3. **Join community groups** — Subtle "looking to buy" posts in school/work networks
4. **Direct approach** — If you see a perfect property not listed, solicitor can contact owner via Land Registry

## Conclusion

Gibraltar's property market rewards **preparation over speed**. The buyers who secure the best properties are those who have their solicitor, mortgage and verification process ready **before** they find the right home. Treat every listing as a starting point for investigation, not a finished product.

*This guide is general information. Always instruct a Gibraltar-qualified solicitor and RICS surveyor for specific transactions.*
