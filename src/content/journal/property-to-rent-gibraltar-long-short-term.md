# Property to Rent in Gibraltar: Long-term vs Short-term Guide 2027

Renting in Gibraltar operates in a tight, high-demand market with distinct dynamics for long-term residential tenancies versus short-term / holiday lets. This guide covers both paths, the legal framework, costs, and practical advice for tenants and landlords.

> **Market Context**: Gibraltar has ~12,000 residential rental units (est. 2026). Vacancy rate <2%. Quality stock moves within days. Tenants should be prepared to act fast with full documentation.

---

## Long-Term Residential Lettings (6+ Months)

### Legal Framework

**Governing Law**: Landlord & Tenant Act (Gibraltar) — similar principles to England & Wales but distinct statute.

**Key Tenant Protections**:
- **Security of tenure** — Assured tenancy after 6 months (landlord needs grounds for possession)
- **Rent control** — No statutory rent caps, but excessive increases challengeable
- **Deposit protection** — No mandatory scheme (unlike UK); negotiate stakeholder holding
- **Repairs** — Landlord responsible for structure, exterior, installations (water, gas, electricity, sanitation)
- **Notice periods** — Minimum 4 weeks (monthly tenancy) or 1 week (weekly); landlord 2 months for Section 21 equivalent

### Typical Tenancy Terms

| Term | Standard Practice |
|------|-------------------|
| **Duration** | 12 months (break clause at 6 months common) |
| **Deposit** | 1–1.5 months' rent (negotiable) |
| **Rent payment** | Monthly in advance (bank transfer) |
| **Bills** | Tenant pays: electricity, water, internet, TV licence. Service charge usually landlord. |
| **Inventory** | Essential — signed at check-in/out with photos |
| **Furnishing** | "Furnished" = white goods + beds + sofa + dining. "Unfurnished" = white goods only. Clarify. |

### Finding a Long-Term Rental

1. **Register with agencies** — Century 21, Chestertons, Property Shop, GTS, Elysium, JYS. Provide: budget, areas, move-in date, employment proof, references.
2. **Monitor Facebook groups** — "Gibraltar Property Rentals", "Gibraltar Flat Shares", "Expats Gibraltar Housing" — daily posts, fast moves.
3. **View efficiently** — Batch by area; bring documents (ID, proof of income, references, bank statements).
4. **Act fast** — Good stock goes in 24–48 hours. Have deposit funds accessible.

### Tenant Checklist at Viewing

- [ ] Test water pressure, hot water, heating/AC
- [ ] Check for damp/mould (especially ground floor, coastal)
- [ ] Verify white goods condition and age
- [ ] Confirm parking allocation (deeded vs permit vs none)
- [ ] Test internet speed (critical for remote work)
- [ ] Ask about service charge inclusions (water? building insurance?)
- [ ] Check sound insulation (neighbour noise at viewing)
- [ ] Verify EPC rating (affects utility costs)
- [ ] Photograph everything for inventory baseline

## Short-Term / Holiday Lets (Under 6 Months)

### Legal Requirements

**Tourism Licence** — **Mandatory** for any let under 28 days (Tourist Board Act). Apply via Gibraltar Tourist Board.
- Fee: £200–£500/year depending on capacity
- Requirements: Fire risk assessment, gas/electric certificates, insurance, waste management plan
- Display: Licence number on all advertisements

**Planning Consent** — Change of use from residential (Class C3) to holiday let (Class C1) **may require Planning permission**. Check with Town Planning pre-application.

**Lease Restrictions** — Many leases (especially marina, newer reclamation) **prohibit short-term lets**. Check lease alienation clause. Freeholder consent required even if lease silent.

**Tax** — Rental income taxable in Gibraltar. Non-resident landlords: 25% withholding tax on gross rent (unless double tax treaty applies).

### Short-Term vs Long-Term: Landlord Comparison

| Factor | Long-Term (12-mo) | Short-Term (Holiday) |
|--------|-------------------|---------------------|
| **Gross yield** | 4.5–5.5% | 6–10% (peak) |
| **Net yield** | 2.5–3.5% | 3–6% (highly variable) |
| **Void risk** | Low (2–4 weeks/year) | High (Nov–Mar 30–50% empty) |
| **Management effort** | Low | High (turnover, cleaning, guests) |
| **Regulatory risk** | Stable | **High** (licensing, planning, lease, tax changes) |
| **Wear & tear** | Low | High |
| **Income predictability** | High | Low (seasonal) |
| **Capital access** | 1-month notice (break clause) | Immediate (block calendar) |

**Verdict**: Short-term lets can outperform **net** but require active management, regulatory compliance, and tolerance for seasonality. Most landlords default to long-term for stability.

## Tenant Rights & Dispute Resolution

### Common Issues & Solutions

| Issue | Tenant Action |
|-------|---------------|
| **Disrepair (damp, leaks, heating)** | Written notice to landlord/agent → 14 days → Environmental Health (GHA) complaint → Rent withholding (legal advice needed) |
| **Deposit not returned** | Written demand → 21 days → Small Claims Court (Magistrates' Court) |
| **Illegal eviction / harassment** | Police (harassment) + solicitor injunction |
| **Rent increase dispute** | Negotiate → Market evidence → Tribunal (if applicable) |
| **Noise / neighbour nuisance** | Landlord/agent → Environmental Health → Court injunction |

## Landlord Compliance Checklist (Long-Term Let)

- [ ] **Gas Safety Certificate** (annual, Gas Safe engineer) — mandatory
- [ ] **Electrical Installation Condition Report** (EICR, 5-yearly) — best practice
- [ ] **Energy Performance Certificate** (EPC, 10-yearly) — mandatory for marketing
- [ ] **Fire risk assessment** (common parts) — if HMO or block
- [ ] **Legionella risk assessment** — recommended
- [ ] **Buildings insurance** (landlord's responsibility)
- [ ] **Deposit handling** — written terms; stakeholder arrangement recommended
- [ ] **Inventory & schedule of condition** — signed at check-in
- [ ] **Tenancy agreement** — written; Gibraltar law; includes: rent, deposit, notice, repairs, access, termination
- [ ] **Right to Rent checks** — not applicable in Gibraltar (but verify identity/immigration status)
- [ ] **Service charge apportionment** — clear in tenancy agreement

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I sublet my rental?
Only with landlord's written consent. Most tenancy agreements prohibit. Check lease if you're a leaseholder landlord — freeholder consent also needed.

### What if the landlord wants to sell?
Tenancy survives sale (buyer becomes landlord). Viewings: reasonable notice (24 hrs), reasonable hours. Tenant not obliged to allow viewings on weekends/evenings unless agreed.

### Can I keep a pet?
Lease/tenancy agreement dependent. Many marina leases prohibit. Newer reclamation often allow with consent. Get written permission.

### How much notice to leave?
**Tenant**: 4 weeks (monthly tenancy) or per break clause.
**Landlord**: 2 months (Section 21 equivalent) + valid grounds if within fixed term.

### What if there's no written tenancy agreement?
Oral tenancy = periodic tenancy (monthly). Same statutory protections apply. But harder to prove terms. Always get written agreement.

### Can I use my rental address for residency/bank account?
Yes — tenancy agreement + utility bill = proof of address for Gibraltar residency, bank account, GP registration.

## Conclusion

Gibraltar's rental market favours **prepared tenants** (documents ready, fast decision) and **compliant landlords** (safety certificates, written agreements, deposit transparency). Long-term lets offer stability; short-term lets offer higher gross returns but with regulatory complexity and operational burden.

**For tenants**: Budget 3–4 months' rent upfront, register with 3+ agencies, view fast, document everything.

**For landlords**: Professional management (10–15% + VAT) pays for itself in compliance, void reduction, and tenant quality. Never skip gas/electric certificates.

*This guide is general information. Landlord & tenant law is fact-specific. Consult a Gibraltar-qualified solicitor for disputes or complex arrangements.*
