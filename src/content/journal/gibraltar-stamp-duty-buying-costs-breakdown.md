# Gibraltar Stamp Duty & Buying Costs: Complete 2027 Breakdown

Buying property in Gibraltar involves a layered cost structure: Stamp Duty (the largest single cost), legal fees, mortgage expenses, surveys, registration and ongoing ownership costs. This guide provides a line-by-line breakdown with 2027 rates, worked examples and budgeting templates.

> **All figures current as of 2027 budget announcements. Stamp Duty rates set by Stamp Duties Act 2005 (as amended). Confirm with your solicitor before committing.**

---

## 1. Stamp Duty (SD) — The Largest Cost

### 2027 Rates (Per Stamp Duties Act 2005, amended 2024 Budget)

| Buyer Category | First £260,000 | £260,001–£350,000 | £350,001–£1,000,000 | £1,000,001+ |
|----------------|----------------|-------------------|---------------------|-------------|
| **First-Time Buyer (Gibraltar Resident)** | **0%** | **2%** | **5.5%** | **10%** |
| **Other Gibraltar Resident** | 2% | 5.5% | 10% | 12% |
| **Non-Resident Individual** | 5% | 7.5% | 10% | 12% |
| **Company / Corporate Vehicle** | 5% | 7.5% | 10% | 12% |

### First-Time Buyer Relief — Eligibility Checklist

- [ ] **Gibraltar resident** (ordinarily resident, not just property owner)
- [ ] **Never owned** residential property anywhere in the world (including inheritance, trust beneficial interest)
- [ ] **Purchasing as main residence** (not buy-to-let, not second home)
- [ ] **Natural person** (not company, trust, partnership)
- [ ] **Intends to occupy** within 6 months of completion

**Couples**: Both must qualify. If one party previously owned, relief lost entirely.

### Stamp Duty Payment & Filing

- **Due**: Within **30 days of completion** (not exchange)
- **Filed by**: Your solicitor (Form SD1 equivalent)
- **Paid to**: Gibraltar Revenue Authority (GRA)
- **Penalties**: 5% after 30 days; 10% after 6 months; interest on late payment
- **No SDLT-style higher rates for additional properties** — but non-resident/company rates act as de facto surcharge

## 4. Survey Costs (Buyer's Survey — Not Lender's Valuation)

| Survey Type | Suitable For | Typical Cost | Turnaround |
|-------------|--------------|--------------|------------|
| **RICS Level 1 (Condition Report)** | New builds (<5 yrs), modern apartments | £350–£500 | 3–5 days |
| **RICS Level 2 (HomeBuyer Report)** | Standard apartments, houses <30 yrs | £500–£800 | 5–7 days |
| **RICS Level 3 (Building Survey)** | Houses, villas, older conversions, any defects suspected | £800–£1,500+ | 7–14 days |
| **Specific defect report** | Damp, structural movement, roof | £300–£600 | 3–5 days |
| **Drainage CCTV** | Houses, older conversions | £200–£400 | 1–2 days |

**Recommendation**: Level 2 for apartments <20 years. Level 3 for all houses, villas, conversions, and any property with suspected issues.

## 8. Tax Considerations Beyond Stamp Duty

| Tax | Gibraltar | Notes |
|-----|-----------|-------|
| **Capital Gains Tax** | **None** | On property disposal |
| **Inheritance Tax** | **None** | No IHT in Gibraltar |
| **Wealth Tax** | **None** | No annual wealth tax |
| **Rental Income Tax** | 0–25% (personal) / 12.5% (company) | Personal: first £14,000 tax-free; then 17%/25%. Company: 12.5% on profits. |
| **VAT on Property** | **Exempt** | Residential letting exempt; commercial standard-rated |
| **Non-Resident Landlord Withholding** | 25% on gross rent | Unless double tax treaty applies; file return to reclaim |

### Double Tax Treaties (Relevant)
- **UK**: Rental income taxable in Gibraltar; credit in UK. CGT exempt in both.
- **Spain**: Rental income taxable in Gibraltar; Spain taxes worldwide income with credit.
- **EU/EEA**: Varies — check specific treaty.

## 10. Frequently Asked Questions

### When is Stamp Duty paid?
Within 30 days of **completion** (not exchange). Solicitor files and pays from completion funds.

### Can I add Stamp Duty to the mortgage?
**No** — Stamp Duty must be paid from own funds. Some lenders allow "cashback" products that effectively offset, but not directly.

### What if I'm buying with a partner who isn't a first-time buyer?
**Relief lost entirely**. Both parties must qualify. Consider sole ownership by qualifying party (legal/tax advice needed).

### Do I pay Stamp Duty on a lease extension?
**No** — Lease extensions are exempt from Stamp Duty (but legal/valuation costs apply).

### What if completion is delayed beyond 30 days?
Solicitor must still file within 30 days of contractual completion date. Late filing = penalties. Ensure solicitor has funds ready.

### Are there any exemptions for charities / non-profits?
Limited — specific statutory exemptions only. Check with GRA.

### Can I claim Stamp Duty as a business expense?
**No** — Capital cost, not revenue. Added to base cost for CGT (but Gibraltar has no CGT).

## Conclusion

The **total cash required** to buy in Gibraltar is typically **purchase price + 3–8%** (Stamp Duty dominant) + **deposit**. First-time resident buyers benefit significantly from the £260,000 zero-rate band. Non-residents and companies face a 5%+ starting rate that materially affects yield calculations.

**Budgeting rule of thumb**: **Purchase price × 1.05** (resident) / **1.08** (non-resident) for total acquisition cost, then add deposit.

Always obtain a **written completion statement** from your solicitor 5+ days before completion showing every pound required.

*This guide is general information. Tax law changes; personal circumstances vary. Consult a Gibraltar-qualified solicitor and tax adviser for your specific transaction.*
