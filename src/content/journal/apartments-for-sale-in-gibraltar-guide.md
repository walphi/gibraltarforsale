# Apartments for Sale in Gibraltar: Ocean Village, Queensway Quay & Beyond 2027

Apartments dominate Gibraltar's housing market — approximately 80% of dwellings and 85% of transactions. From marina-front penthouses to converted Town Area flats, the range is wide but the due-diligence framework is consistent. This guide breaks down the apartment segments, pricing, service charge economics and the specific checks apartment buyers must make.

| 2-bed | 75–95 m² | £650K–£950K | 4.5–5.0% |

| Penthouse | 150–250 m² | £1.5M–£2.5M | 3.5–4.0% |

**Service Charges (2026 actuals)**:
- 1-bed: £4,200–£5,000/year
- 2-bed: £5,000–£6,500/year
- 3-bed: £6,500–£8,000/year

**Watch Points**: Service charge inflation (5–8% annually typical); noise from bars/restaurants (blocks facing Casino/Leisure Centre); leasehold consent for subletting; sinking fund adequacy for cladding/façade works.

## 3. Town Area & Upper Town Conversions

### Typical Conversion Profile
- **Original use**: Warehouses, offices, barracks, tenements
- **Conversion era**: 1980s–2010s
- **Tenure**: **Complex** — often Crown leasehold with short unexpired terms (50–80 years), some freehold, some flying freehold
- **Building quality**: Highly variable

### Pricing (2026 asking)
| Area | 1-Bed | 2-Bed | 3-Bed |
|------|-------|-------|-------|
| Main Street / Casemates | £300K–£450K | £450K–£650K | £650K–£900K |
| Irish Town / Library Ramp | £320K–£480K | £480K–£700K | £700K–£950K |
| Upper Town (Castle Road, etc.) | £350K–£500K | £500K–£750K | £750K–£1.0M |

### Service Charges
- **Wide range**: £1,500–£4,500/year
- Often **no sinking fund** — major works = special levy
- Management: Often self-managed or small local managing agents
- Insurance: May be separate from service charge

### Critical Due Diligence for Conversions

1. **Leasehold term** — **#1 risk**. Many conversions on 99-year leases from 1920s–1950s. Unexpired term 50–75 years.
   - Mortgage: Most lenders require >70 years at completion + 30 years beyond term
   - Extension: Statutory route possible but Crown Estate involvement adds cost/time
   - Value impact: <70 years = 15–25% discount to long-lease equivalent

2. **Structural alterations** — Conversions often involved:
   - Floor insertion (mezzanines)
   - Window/door openings in load-bearing walls
   - Roof extensions
   - **Verify**: Building Control completion certificates for ALL works

3. **Fire safety** — Older conversions may not meet current standards
   - Compartmentation between units
   - Common area fire doors, alarms, escape routes
   - External wall systems (cladding/insulation)

4. **Sound insulation** — Party walls/floors in conversions often sub-standard
   - Test at viewing (neighbour noise, impact sound)
   - EPC may reveal insulation gaps

5. **Service charge transparency** — Small schemes often lack professional management
   - Request 3 years accounts + sinking fund status
   - Check for upcoming major works (roof, lift, façade)

## Service Charge Deep Dive: What You're Paying For

| Component | Typical % of Total | Marina Luxury | Modern Reclamation | Conversions |
|-----------|-------------------|---------------|-------------------|-------------|
| Building insurance | 20–30% | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ (sometimes separate) |
| Management fee | 10–15% | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Cleaning / common areas | 10–15% | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Lift maintenance | 5–10% | ✓ | ✓ | If lift |
| Concierge / security | 15–25% | ✓ | Rare | No |
| Marina / external upkeep | 10–20% | ✓ | No | No |
| Pool / gym | 5–10% | ✓ | Some | Rare |
| Sinking fund / reserve | 10–20% | ✓ | ✓ | Often **missing** |
| **Total (2-bed example)** | **100%** | **£5,500** | **£3,200** | **£2,500** |

### Questions to Ask Before Buying

1. **What is the current sinking fund balance?** (Should be 10–15% of rebuild cost for mature blocks)
2. **Are there any planned major works in next 3 years?** (Roof, lifts, façade, cladding, M&E)
3. **Has a Section 20 / major works consultation been issued recently?**
4. **What is the service charge history (3–5 years)?** — Look for >5% annual increases
5. **Who is the managing agent?** — Professional (Savills, Chestertons, etc.) vs self-managed
6. **Is buildings insurance included?** — If not, add £500–£1,500 to your budget
7. **Are there any arrears or litigation?** — Ask for management accounts

## Leasehold Checklist for Apartment Buyers

| Check | Why It Matters | Action |
|-------|----------------|--------|
| Unexpired lease term | <70 years = mortgage/value issues | Get term in writing; factor extension cost |
| Ground rent amount & review | Escalating rent = future cost / saleability | Request lease copy; check review mechanism |
| Service charge provisions | Caps? Transparency? Sinking fund? | Read lease Schedule; request accounts |
| Alienation (subletting) consent | Required for renting out | Check lease; get freeholder policy |
| Alterations consent | Required for any works | Check lease; budget for consent fees |
| Buildings insurance | Who arranges? Adequate cover? | Confirm in lease; check sum insured |
| Major works consultation rights | Section 20 equivalent protections | Verify lease provides consultation rights |
| Freeholder identity & responsiveness | Crown Estate / LPS vs private | Ask agent; check LPS track record |

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I buy an apartment with a 60-year lease?
Possible but **difficult to finance**. Cash buyers only, or specialist lenders at lower LTV. Value significantly discounted. Extension possible but Crown Estate process adds £15K–£30K+ costs and 6–12 months.

### What's the difference between "service charge" and "ground rent"?
- **Service charge**: Annual payment for building/services (insurance, cleaning, lifts, management, reserves)
- **Ground rent**: Annual payment to freeholder for the land lease (often peppercorn/£50–£300, but can escalate)

### Do I need a survey for a new-build apartment?
**Yes** — Snagging survey (£300–£500) identifies defects for developer to fix under warranty. RICS Level 2 for conversions/older.

### Can I rent out my apartment on Airbnb?
**Tourism Licence required** from Gibraltar Tourist Board. Lease may prohibit short-term lets. Planning consent may be needed for change of use. Check all three before listing.

### What happens if the sinking fund is empty?
Major works = **special levy** (one-off demand on all leaseholders). Can be £5K–£50K+ per unit. Always check sinking fund balance pre-purchase.

### Are pets allowed?
Lease-dependent. Many marina leases restrict pets. Newer reclamation leases often allow with consent. Get written confirmation.

## Conclusion

Gibraltar apartments offer **liquidity, rental yield and lower maintenance burden** than houses — but the **service charge economics and leasehold terms** are the true cost drivers. The best apartment purchase is one where:

1. **Lease term >100 years** (or clear, affordable extension path)
2. **Service charge is transparent, with healthy sinking fund**
3. **Management is professional and responsive**
4. **Building insurance is included and adequate**
5. **Rental restrictions (if investing) align with your strategy**

Commission a solicitor who **specialises in Gibraltar leasehold apartments** — the nuances of Crown Estate freeholds, LPS consents and local managing agents require specific experience.
