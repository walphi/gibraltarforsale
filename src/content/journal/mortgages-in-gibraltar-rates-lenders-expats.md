# Mortgages in Gibraltar: Rates, Lenders & Eligibility for Expats 2027

Securing a mortgage in Gibraltar differs from the UK or Spain in key ways: a concentrated lender market, conservative loan-to-value caps, Sterling/Euro dual-currency pricing, and specific eligibility rules for non-residents. This guide covers the 2027 landscape for buyers financing a Gibraltar property purchase.

> **Market Context**: ~£1.2B total mortgage book (est. 2026). 4 main active lenders. 65–70% of purchases involve a mortgage. Average LTV: 65% (resident), 55% (non-resident).

| Buyer Category | Max LTV | Typical Max Loan | Notes |

| **UK Resident / Expat (UK Income)** | 65% | £1M–£1.5M | Sterling income; UK credit check |
| **EU/EEA Resident (Euro Income)** | 60–65% | €1M–€1.5M | Euro income; local credit check |
| **Other Non-Resident** | 55–60% | £750K–£1M | Case-by-case; higher rates |
| **Corporate / SPV** | 55–60% | £1M | Personal guarantees required |
| **New Build (Off-Plan)** | 60–65% | Per lender policy | Stage payments; long-stop dates |

**Key constraint**: Gibraltar lenders **do not lend above 75% LTV** on residential. No 90% or 95% products exist. Deposit must be **25%+ for residents, 35–45%+ for non-residents**.

## Eligibility Criteria Deep Dive

### Income Assessment

| Income Type | Treatment | Evidence Required |
|-------------|-----------|-------------------|
| **Employed (Gibraltar)** | 100% of basic + guaranteed overtime/bonus | 3 months payslips, P60, employer reference |
| **Employed (UK/Overseas)** | 100% basic; variable case-by-case | 3 months payslips, contract, tax return |
| **Self-Employed (Sole Trader)** | Net profit × 1 (latest year) or average 2–3 yrs | 2–3 yrs SA302s, accounts, accountant cert |
| **Company Director** | Salary + dividends (avg 2–3 yrs) | 2–3 yrs accounts, SA302s, accountant cert |
| **Pension Income** | 100% (if guaranteed) | Pension statements, award letters |
| **Rental Income** | 70–80% of gross (void/maintenance deduction) | Tenancy agreements, bank statements |
| **Investment Income** | Case-by-case | Portfolio statements, tax returns |

**Affordability Multiple**: Typically **4.0–4.5x gross income** (post-tax for non-UK). Stressed at **5.5–6.0%** interest rate.

### Credit & Background

- **UK credit check** (Experian/Equifax/TransUnion) for UK residents/expats
- **Gibraltar Credit Reference Agency** for local residents
- **International credit report** for other non-residents (cost: £50–£150, buyer pays)
- **AML/KYC**: Source of deposit, source of wealth, PEPS/sanctions screening
- **Minimum age**: 18 | **Maximum age at term end**: 70–75 (varies)

### Property Eligibility

| Property Type | Lendable? | Conditions |
|---------------|-----------|------------|
| Standard apartment (leasehold >70 yrs) | Yes | Standard |
| Apartment (leasehold 50–70 yrs) | Restricted | Max 60% LTV; shorter term |
| Apartment (leasehold <50 yrs) | **No** | Unmortgageable |
| House / Villa (freehold/long lease) | Yes | Standard |
| House (short lease) | Restricted | As above |
| New build (off-plan) | Yes | Stage release; warranty required |
| Conversion (no Building Control sign-off) | **No** | Regularisation required first |
| Flying freehold / complex title | Case-by-case | Specialist valuer |
| Commercial / mixed-use | **No** (residential mortgage) | Commercial loan needed |

## Application Process & Timeline

### Step-by-Step

| Stage | Timeline | Buyer Action |
|-------|----------|--------------|
| **1. Decision in Principle (DIP)** | 24–48 hrs | Submit: ID, income docs, deposit proof, property details |
| **2. Full Application** | 1–2 weeks | Complete form; pay valuation fee (£300–£600); submit full docs |
| **3. Valuation** | 1–2 weeks | Lender instructs valuer; buyer pays; report to lender |
| **4. Underwriting** | 1–2 weeks | Credit check, affordability verification, property assessment |
| **5. Mortgage Offer** | Valid 3–6 months | Formal offer with conditions; review with solicitor |
| **6. Completion** | Per purchase timeline | Solicitor requests funds; lender releases (3–5 days' notice) |

**Total DIP → Offer**: **3–5 weeks** typical. Complex cases (self-employed, non-resident, complex title): 6–8 weeks.

### Documents Checklist (Prepare Early)

- [ ] Passport (certified copy)
- [ ] Proof of address (utility bill <3 months)
- [ ] 3 months bank statements (all accounts)
- [ ] 3 months payslips / 2–3 yrs SA302s + accounts
- [ ] P60 or tax overview
- [ ] Proof of deposit (savings statements, gift letter + donor ID)
- [ ] Existing mortgage statements (if porting)
- [ ] Credit report (UK: checkmyfile/Experian; Gibraltar: local)
- [ ] Property details (address, price, agent, tenure)
- [ ] Solicitor details (name, firm, contact)

## Cross-Border Considerations

### Sterling vs Euro Mortgage

| Factor | GBP Mortgage | EUR Mortgage |
|--------|--------------|--------------|
| **Income match** | UK/GBP earners | Eurozone earners |
| **Rate benchmark** | BoE Base Rate | EURIBOR |
| **Current rate advantage** | Slightly higher | Slightly lower |
| **Currency risk** | None (if GBP income) | None (if EUR income) |
| **Switching** | Possible (fees apply) | Possible (fees apply) |
| **Early repayment** | ERCs per product | ERCs per product |

**Recommendation**: Match mortgage currency to **primary income currency**. If dual income, consider split (e.g., 70% GBP / 30% EUR) — not all lenders offer.

### Non-Resident Specifics

| Issue | Gibraltar Approach |
|-------|-------------------|
| **UK credit history** | Used for UK residents; Experian/Equifax/TransUnion |
| **Proof of overseas income** | Translated accounts, tax returns, bank statements |
| **Deposit from overseas** | Allowed; source of funds evidence required (6+ months trail) |
| **Tax residency** | Lender checks; affects rate/product eligibility |
| **Repayment from overseas** | SWIFT/SEPA; some lenders require Gibraltar account |
| **Inheritance / succession** | Gibraltar law applies to property; wills advised |

### Brexit / Treaty Impact

- **Current**: UK lenders (NatWest Intl, Lloyds) operate under passporting/TPR permissions
- **Treaty ratification**: Could ease cross-border lending, expand Euro product range
- **No treaty**: Status quo; UK credit data sharing continues via adequacy decisions

## Mortgage Alternatives

### 1. Private Bank / Wealth Management Lending
- **Providers**: Jyske Private Banking, NatWest International Premier, local family offices
- **Flexibility**: Bespoke terms, interest-only, Lombard lending against portfolio
- **Minimum**: £500K–£1M+ relationship
- **Rate**: Negotiated; often BoE/EURIBOR + 1.0–1.5%

### 2. Vendor Finance / Seller Carry-Back
- **Structure**: Seller lends 10–20% of price, 2–5 year term, 4–6% interest
- **Use case**: Buyer short on deposit; bridge to mainstream remortgage
- **Legal**: Requires solicitor for both parties; register charge at Land Registry

### 3. Bridge Loan
- **Providers**: Specialist bridge lenders (UK/Gibraltar), some private banks
- **Term**: 3–18 months
- **Rate**: 0.75–1.25%/month + 1–2% arrangement
- **Use case**: Chain break, auction, off-plan stage payment
- **Exit**: Mainstream remortgage or sale

### 4. Family / Gifted Deposit
- **Structure**: Gift letter + donor ID + bank trail (6+ months)
- **Lender view**: Accepted by all; no repayment obligation
- **Tax**: No Gibraltar gift tax; check donor's jurisdiction

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I get a mortgage if I'm paid in crypto / freelance / gig economy?
**Very difficult**. Lenders need stable, evidenced income. Crypto income generally not accepted. Freelance: 2–3 yrs accounts required.

### What if I have a UK mortgage already?
**Porting** possible with same lender (NatWest, etc.) if affordability passes. Otherwise, new application. Two mortgages = combined affordability assessment.

### Can I get interest-only?
**Residential**: Rare. Only for high-net-worth with clear repayment vehicle (investment portfolio, pension lump sum). **Buy-to-Let**: Common (70–75% LTV interest-only).

### What happens if rates rise after I fix?
Fixed rate = protected for fixed period. Tracker = moves with BoE/EURIBOR. Stress test at 5.5–6% before committing.

### Can I overpay?
Most fixed products allow **10% of balance per year** without ERC. Tracker: usually unlimited. Check product terms.

### Do I need life insurance?
**Not mandatory** but strongly advised. Lender will offer; compare independent. Critical illness cover also recommended.

### Can a non-resident get a mortgage without visiting Gibraltar?
**Yes** — DIP and application remote. Completion via solicitor with Power of Attorney. Valuation instructs locally.

### What if the valuation comes in low?
Options: (1) Renegotiate price, (2) Increase deposit to maintain LTV, (3) Challenge valuation with comparable evidence, (4) Walk away (if pre-offer DIP).

## Conclusion

Gibraltar mortgages are **available but conservative**: 65–75% LTV, income multiples 4–4.5x, stress-tested at 5.5–6%. The market rewards **preparation** — clean credit, documented income, proven deposit, and a Gibraltar-qualified solicitor on the lender's panel.

**Non-residents and expats**: Expect 60–65% max LTV, higher rates, and more documentation. Match currency to income. Allow 6–8 weeks for full offer.

**Best strategy**: Secure a **Decision in Principle before property hunting**. It proves seriousness to agents, locks rate for 60–90 days, and reveals affordability ceiling.

*This guide is general information. Mortgage criteria change; individual circumstances vary. Consult a whole-of-market broker or lender directly for current terms.*
