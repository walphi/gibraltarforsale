# Gibraltar Property Market Update

## Current update: no figures published

This page does not publish a numerical update for the current quarter. No documented, date-bounded sample of Gibraltar property listings or completed transactions has been supplied for this edition. Without that evidence, a transaction count, price movement, average, or claim about whether the market is rising or falling would be guesswork. No such figures or market claims are made here.

This is a statement about the evidence available for this page, not a claim that no property data exists. Gibraltar’s Government has previously said that real estate data in its Business and OFT annual report came from annual returns submitted by licensed real estate agents and covered the 2024 calendar year. That is a specified annual source and period, not a current-quarter dataset. [1]

## What a quarterly update will report

A future edition will state its exact reporting period and publication date, then describe its evidence before presenting results. The core measures should include the number of observed listings and, if a verifiable source permits, completed sales; asking prices separately from achieved prices; and the property types and areas represented. Any price measure should explain whether it is a median, mean, range, or another calculation, what units it uses, and how many observations support it. A small or uneven sample should be labelled as such rather than presented as a market-wide result.

The update should identify where records came from, when they were collected, and whether the source is an official return, a transaction record, or advertised asking prices. Those sources do not measure the same thing. A listing is an offer to sell, not proof of a completed sale or its final price. Where multiple adverts appear to describe one property, duplicates, withdrawn adverts, and records with missing dates or prices should be checked and handled consistently. If that cannot be done, the limitation should be stated.

Comparisons should use clearly named periods and consistent definitions. A quarter-on-quarter comparison can be affected by seasonal timing or a thin sample; a year-on-year comparison also needs comparable coverage in both periods. If the evidence does not support a reliable comparison, the page should say so instead of filling the gap with an explanation. It should not attribute a movement to interest rates, policy, supply, or buyer behaviour without evidence that supports that link.

Land Property Services Limited (LPS) lists land registration, stamp duty, rent assessor, rating, valuation, and land-related functions among its services. [2] These are relevant official channels for particular records or property questions, but the existence of a service does not itself provide a complete, public, quarterly market series. A future update will link the exact source records used and note any access or coverage limits.

## How to use this page as a buyer or owner

Treat this page as a guide to what has—and has not—been established, not as a valuation of a particular home. For a property you are considering, useful questions include: Is the advertised price an asking price or a completed-sale figure? When was the information recorded? Does the stated floor area use a consistent basis? What is included in the sale, and what recurring building or service costs apply? Are there restrictions, repair needs, or planned works that need separate review?

These are due-diligence prompts, not statements that a particular cost, restriction, or condition applies. Ask for documents that answer the questions and have the relevant details checked by an appropriately qualified local professional. LPS describes itself as a Gibraltar property-advisory service to Government and lists its public-facing property functions; it is one official point of reference, not a substitute for advice on an individual purchase. ## Frequently asked questions

### Does “no numerical update” mean prices are unchanged?

No. It means this edition lacks a documented current-quarter sample from which to calculate or verify a movement. It is not evidence of either a rise, a fall, or no change.

### Why not use online asking prices as the market price?

Asking prices can help describe advertised supply if sampled consistently, but they do not establish what a buyer paid. Any future listing-based measure will be labelled as asking-price evidence and kept separate from verified completed-sale data.

### When will figures be added?

Only when a source, sample, and time period can be documented and the measures can be described clearly. If a suitable dataset is not available for a quarter, the page will continue to say so rather than imply precision it cannot support.
