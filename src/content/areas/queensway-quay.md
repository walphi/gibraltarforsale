# Queensway Quay Property for Sale

## Overview

Queensway Quay is a compact waterfront neighbourhood built around a working marina on Gibraltar’s western harbour side. The Government visitor map places Queensway Quay Marina along Queensway, among the harbourfront landmarks and within reach of the older city centre, Ocean Village and Commonwealth Park. [1] Government planning minutes also refer to the Queensway Quay residential development. [5] The appeal is specific: a waterside outlook and a town setting, rather than a quiet, out-of-town residential suburb.

A marina address alone does not tell you what a home feels like day to day. One apartment may look directly over the berths; another may face the road, a neighbouring building or an internal aspect. The building, floor, outlook and exact route to the entrance matter as much as the district name. Treat view, sunlight, noise and privacy as features to verify at the individual property, not assumptions about every home in Queensway Quay.

## Property types and price context

The area is best approached as a search for apartment living around a marina, rather than a neighbourhood of detached houses. Within that broad category, compare each building and unit on its own merits: layout, usable outdoor space, storage, lift access, parking arrangements, orientation and the condition of shared areas. Confirm which features belong to the apartment, which are shared, and which—if any—are managed separately by the marina.

No asking-price range or sale-price statistic is included here. A figure would need to be supported by recent, comparable transactions or clearly dated, current listings, with differences in floor area, condition, aspect and building accounted for. Ask for recent evidence relevant to the exact block and unit, and distinguish completed-sale information from asking prices. Similarly, no rental yield is quoted: any estimate should use a documented achievable rent and the buyer’s full ownership and operating costs, rather than a headline rent alone.

This guide does not verify live property listings or current availability. Availability changes, so confirm that a property is still on the market and check its particulars, asking price and inclusions directly with the responsible agent or seller. Do not infer that a building or unit is available merely because it appears in older marketing.

## Marina character and local amenities

The marina operator describes Queensway Quay as a yacht marina and lists marina-specific services, including berth-holder facilities. [2] [3] These are not automatically amenities for apartment owners or residents. Before relying on a gym, pool, parking space, storage, concierge, marina access or any other feature, ask whether it exists in the residential building, whether it is included with the specific home, and what terms govern its use. The operator also describes eateries around the marina, but opening hours and businesses can change; check what is currently operating rather than treating a directory or brochure as a guarantee. [3]

For wider everyday context, the Government map marks Commonwealth Park, Westside Park, Ocean Village, supermarkets and central-city landmarks in the same part of Gibraltar. [1] The Citibus route information identifies a Queensway Quay stop and lists connections through nearby central areas and amenities. [4] This supports checking the neighbourhood on foot and by public transport, but it is not a promise of a particular travel time, service frequency or step-free route. Confirm current schedules and walk the route at the times you expect to travel.

## Transport and commute

Queensway runs beside the marina, and the official visitor map shows it connecting into the central road network. [1] A bus stop named Queensway Quay appears in Citibus route information. [4] For a commute, check the current route, first and last services, any transfer, and the stop that best serves your destination. If you drive, inspect the actual access, parking allocation and visitor arrangements for the building; do not assume that a waterfront address includes a private space or that nearby parking is available to residents.

The harbour setting is also worth testing in person. Visit at different times and, where possible, with windows open and closed. Listen for road, restaurant, vessel and event activity; check wind and sun on balconies; and ask how salt exposure is addressed in exterior finishes, windows and shared maintenance. These are practical inspection questions, not claims that every apartment experiences the same conditions.

## Balanced view: who may value it, and what to weigh

Queensway Quay may suit a buyer who values a marina outlook, a central location and the possibility of reaching city amenities without making every trip by car. Its waterfront character is its defining benefit. In exchange, a buyer should be comfortable assessing a busier harbour setting, the precise outlook and exposure, and the running arrangements of a particular building rather than relying on a neighbourhood-wide promise.

Before making an offer, request the title and plan information, building and shared-area records, recent maintenance history, insurance details, any applicable service-charge or reserve-fund documentation, parking terms, and written confirmation of what is included in the sale. Ask about planned works, defects, noise arrangements and any restrictions that could affect use or letting. Verify the answers with the relevant documents and qualified advisers; do not assume that a marina’s fees, access rules or facilities apply to residential ownership.

## Nearby areas

Ocean Village lies nearby on the harbourfront, while Commonwealth Park and the southern city-centre streets offer a different, more urban setting. The Government map shows these as adjoining parts of central Gibraltar. [1] Compare them in person: a short map distance may feel different depending on crossings, gradients, building entrances and the route you actually use.

## FAQs

### Is Queensway Quay a marina or a residential area?

It is a marina-centred waterfront location with residential property in the surrounding development. The marina operator’s services are described for marina use, so verify separately what a residential building provides. [2] [3] [5]

### Are there homes for sale now, and what do they cost?

This page does not check live listings or quote a price. Ask for current availability and comparable evidence for the exact building and apartment; asking prices are not completed-sale prices.

### Can I rely on a bus for commuting?

Citibus lists a Queensway Quay stop, but routes and timetables can change. Check the current operator information and test the journey for your own destination and schedule. [4]

### What legal, tax, residency or mortgage checks are needed?

These subjects depend on individual circumstances and current rules. This page is general property information, not legal, tax, residency or mortgage advice. Obtain advice from appropriately qualified Gibraltar professionals before relying on any conclusion.
